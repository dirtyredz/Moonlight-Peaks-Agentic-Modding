# The Moonlight Peaks Mod Landscape

Snapshot taken **2026-08-02** from [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/games/moonlightpeaks/mods),
sorted by all-time downloads. Nexus is effectively the only distribution hub right now —
no Thunderstore or Steam Workshop presence.

## Scene size and shape

- **88 mods total**, first uploads dated 2026-06-22 (pre-launch/press build), launch was 2026-07-07.
- Category split: Gameplay 49, Visuals 11, User Interface 9, Cheats 6, Utilities 4,
  Miscellaneous 4, Characters 2, Crafting 2, Items 1.
- Top mod has ~3.9k downloads. For comparison, a mature scene's top mod is in the millions.
  **This is a ground-floor scene** — a decent QoL mod today lands in the top 20 within a week.
- Roughly a dozen authors produce nearly everything. `SerenaEnchanted`, `MissLarifari1`,
  `roguechikkin`, `Elsiabeth`, `CostMillion`, and `lockyaw` account for most of the top list.

## What the numbers say

**Four clusters dominate:**

1. **Inventory/logistics friction** — Craft from Chests, House Storage Anywhere, Stack to
   Storage. Classic cozy-farmsim pain points; these always chart.
2. **Information the game hides** — Extra Tooltip, Detailed Minimap, Quest Tracker,
   My Little Black Book. Highest endorsement-to-download ratios in the scene, which suggests
   people *keep* them rather than trying and uninstalling.
3. **Time/tedium removal** — Faster Planting, Batch Cooking, Auto Pet, Barn Butler,
   FasterGrowth, Faster Machines.
4. **Cosmetics** — portrait replacers are the whole Visuals category basically. The fact that
   "Serena's Portrait Replacer" is a *framework* (drop PNGs in a folder) rather than a
   content pack is the interesting bit.

**Frameworks are emerging, which is the healthy sign.** Two exist already:
- *Serena's Enchanted Studio and Texture Framework* (#1 overall)
- *Fippsie's Texture Swap Framework* (newer; "Rain's Revamped Vanilla Portraits (Fippsie's TSF)" targets it)
- *Mod Menu* by Elsiabeth (691 DL) adds a Mods button to the pause menu so BepInEx mods can
  be configured in-game instead of by editing `.cfg` files.

If we build anything, **supporting Mod Menu / BepInEx ConfigurationManager for settings is
the current community convention** and worth doing from day one.

## Visible gaps (candidate ideas)

- Only **1 mod** in the Items category and **2** in Characters — new *content* is barely
  touched, because it's the hard path (needs Unity + Addressables).
- No large "expansion"-style mods yet: no new NPCs, no new map areas, no new festivals.
- Localization only covers FR/ES/PL/PT.
- No mod manager integration listed as Vortex-supported for most mods.
- Nothing doing automation/scheduling beyond the barn (no crop-tending automation, no
  shipping/selling automation).

## Health warning on sources

Several sites surfaced in searches — `plitch.com`, `xmodhub.com`, `modsportal.com`, and
various "Moonlight Peaks Wiki" lookalike domains — are SEO/aggregator sites, some serving
paid "trainers" rather than mods. **Nexus Mods and `moonlightpeaks.wiki.gg` are the real
sources.** Don't download game-adjacent executables from the aggregators.
